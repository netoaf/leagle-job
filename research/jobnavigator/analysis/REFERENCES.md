## Interpretation

These sources establish recognized practices for secure development,
component inventory, provenance and repository risk signals. They do not
provide a formal certification of this intake or of JobNavigator.

The conclusions in this workspace are tied to the exact source commit,
documented local checks and explicit limitations. Automated scores and the
presence of a security policy are supporting evidence, not proof that software
is secure.
